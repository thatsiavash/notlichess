### Deviations and decisions
- **Return shape.** `boardOptsFor` returns the whole frame, not only `opts`: the spoiler rule needs the position (whose piece slides) and the bar (`pending`). `live` and `pending` moved in as data because they are part of what the board shows; `renderCardBoard` only applies them.
- **`xpSpoil` memo.** In explore frames `boardOptsFor` calls `xpSpoil`, which caches `a.preKey` on its first call. It is an idempotent cache, not state; left as is, and the purity test ignores `preKey`.
- **"check skipped".** Read as: skip the check-phase frame, not the 28 check cards. The test opens every card in guess (as `session.test.js`'s `OPEN` does, plus `a.animMove = a.lastMove` so the open frame matches a plain guess card), so all 91 cards get guess frames. Slice 2 can drop that forcing once the check is gone.
- **Hint frames** set `a.hints` to 0, 1, 2 directly instead of calling `giveHint`, whose tier-3 gate (removed in slice 6) would leave tier-3 hint frames untested.
- **The checker goes slightly beyond 2.3's literal list**, all in its spirit: a gold arrow (Stockfish's pick, explore only) is forbidden before an answer; the bar must be waiting; the board's `aria-label` must not contain the answer's SAN. Exempt as in 2.3: the red game arrow, last-move tints, selection and dots, the player's own drawn shapes.
- **Verification beyond the tests:** a scratch script drew every fixture card at tiers 1 to 3 through guess, selection, hints, checking, the answer shown, all three lines stepped forward and back, a solve, and exploring (start, result, a move, the second row), once with the HEAD build and once with the new one: 8,938 frames, identical board markup, static class, bar class and height, and identical `lastView` / `animMove` / `explore.lastEv` / `explore.anim` after each draw. Real page at 390x844 (aidanmilne): open, selection, hint 1, hint 2, answer shown, refute line, explore all draw identical board markup before and after. Browser e2e at 1280x900: 53 passed, 0 failed.

### Deviations and decisions
- **Card 1 is never forcing, by rule, not only by ease.** The spec gives "ease ties put non-forcing cards first; at most one forcing card in the first three" and the test "card 1 is not forcing". Ease ties alone do not guarantee card 1 when a forcing card has strictly higher ease, so the head pass also keeps card 1 one-move (it falls back to a forcing card only when no one-move card is left).
- **A skipped `lostLast` keeps its place in the ease order**, as the existing comment says for a hard latest loss ("the easiest card leads instead, not an older loss"); it is not moved to card 2.
- **`looksForcing` is a superset of `cardFor`'s rule:** it cannot see `cls.bSettle >= 3` or the trimmed `a.sol.length >= 3`, so a few cards it calls forcing ask for one move (10 of 20 at tier 2 on the fixture). That only delays those cards; the test asserts it never misses a real forcing card. It reads `lu[0]` (the classifier's `bestLine.nodes[1]`), not `b.bu`.
- **The opponent-tap test asserts no grade, not silence:** slice 6 adds T4 text on that tap, which this test allows. A selected piece of yours that can capture the tapped piece makes a move (S2's legal-target rule), so those taps are skipped.
- **Relearn:** with the `!a.check1` gate gone, every `fail` or `retry` queues one more try (as all non-check cards already did). On ajsharabyani (tier 1, 3-card session) the revealed card 1 came back as card 3.

### Deviations and decisions
- **Width table:** 14 px of padding plus the 2 px border (so the words never touch it): "takes" is 49 x 18 on a phone, 55 x 20 on the desktop.
- **Candidates:** the 8 neighbours nearest first (above, below, right, left, then the diagonals), head then tail; in each, the pill centred on the square, then flush with its left edge, then its right (a two-word pill is wider than a square, so centred alone almost never fits beside pieces).
- **Shaft clearance** counts the arrow's halo (stroke + 2.4) plus 4 px each side, and the arrowhead too; the test checks the spec's shaft width plus 4 px.
- **No label while a piece slides** (principle 2); it comes with the land beat, the marks beat or the words.
- **The label is placed again in the words' beat** too: a word for a tap (T4, N1) that takes its room drops it for those 2.5 s; a frame whose piece slid in with no land beat gets its label with its words.
- **"lost" only on See it's token** (copy L); a lost token elsewhere (the story) has its first-sight label only. Won tokens have none.
- **S0's threat** reads from the game's position and shows only while what it took is still drawn there or as the ghost; otherwise nothing (its words would not be true to the board).
- **The prize's "free {piece}" almost never shows**: hint 1's band (4 + 5) beside [Hint 2] [Show the answer] (5) leaves 1 word, so the two-word label is the first thing to go (principle 5). Seen on the real page: no prize label at hint 1. Hint 1's danger label ("takes", one word) fits.
- **Explore's label** is "the computer" at tier 1, as slice 12 made every explore word at tier 1; "Stockfish" at tiers 2 and 3.
- **Worked example:** only where hint 1 draws something (a family whose card draws nothing waits for one that does); the family counter goes up only when a worked example finishes (solved or revealed); the band says the task (T1, T2) until a miss, then the hint's words beside its marks; the button reads "Hint". It is made in `loadCard`, so `cardFor` stays free of storage reads.
- **First sight held in its state:** a first-sight label marked seen at its first paint still counts as unseen for the rest of that state (`tipHeld`), so a selection does not swap it for the next label; the next state shows the next one.
- **The desktop size** follows `matchMedia('(min-width:861px)')`, the stylesheet's own breakpoint.
